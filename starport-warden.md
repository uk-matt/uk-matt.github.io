# Starport Warden
## Introduction
A solo game about managing a small, out of the way starport. Perhaps you can grow it into a big starport before you retire?
The game requires a standard deck of playing cards, two six-sided dice and a way to record the events - paper, pencil, spreadsheet? You will draw one card each year of game time, to represent how business goes that year. Every ten years you will see if the starport has grown or shrunk. After fifty years (or earlier if you get to Class A status) you will retire. This is version 0.1 (March 2026)

## Skills
You have six skill points to divide between these five skills:
* Admin - how good are you at paperwork? From 0 (baffled) to 3 (skilled bureaucrat, amazing at logistics, managing budgets, etc)
* Carousing - are you sociable? From 0 (no fun at all) to 3 (perfect host and fascinating raconteur)
* Liaison - the art of the deal. From 0 (bumbling fool) to 3 (you make every proposal seem win-win)
* Streetwise - dealing with the shadier side of the business. From 0 (quite naive) to 3 (used to dealing with pirates and smugglers)
* Tactics - preparing for trouble and dealing with it when it arises. From 0 (unprepared) to 3 (highly skilled in both planning defences and managing a crisis)

## The starport status
### Facilities
#### Class X starport
This starport barely exists. It has no facilities
#### Class E starport
This starport has a marked out patch of flat land
#### Class D starport
This starport has everything a class E starport has, plus:
* First aid station
* Fuel tank – unrefined
#### Class C starport
This starport has everything a class D starport has, plus:
* Repair facilities – basic
* Medical facility with nurse
* Life support restocking
#### Class B starport
This starport has everything a class C starport has, plus:
* Shipyard – spaceship construction
* Fuel tank – refined
* Medical facility with doctor
#### Class A starport
This starport has everything a class B starport has, plus:
* Repair facilities – refitting & annual maintenance
* Refitting facilities – jump drives
* Shipyard – starship construction

### Reputation
The starport is the front door of the system. If it is well run and stable, it is likely to be well used and grow quickly. If things often go wrong, it will be harder to get business.

### Traffic
A measure of how many starships are using the starport. More visitors means more berth fees, and more customers for other facilities at the starport.

## Gameplay

Each year, draw a card to simulate the main event affecting the starport. Often a face card will result in a skill check. The "effect" of the roll is the difference between the number needed to succeed (which is 8) and the number rolled. As an example, if eleven was rolled for a skill check the effect would be 3 (11-8)
AFter ten cards have been drawn, review a decade of activity. 
* If traffic is 20 or more, there are sufficient funds for the starport to construct a new facility, choose one from the list above that is needed to bring the starport to the next category; and
* If reputation is 10 or more, add a point to one of your skills.

### Number cards
Represent 
* Clubs - criminal and pirate activity harms the starport, from stealing cargo from the occasional trader to attacks so frequent they discourage merchants from visiting the system at all. Reduce traffic and reputation scores by the number on the card (split between the two)
* Diamonds - business optimism. The starport thrives from local trading conditions, political and financial stablity, citizen morale. Raise reputation score by the number on the card
* Hearts - population increases and so more imports and exports go through the starport, along with additional passengers. Raise traffic score by the number on the card
* Spades - business activity on the mainworld increases, which means activity at the starport increases. Raise traffic and reputation scores by the number on the card (split between the two)

### Face cards
Represent a person or organisation which will probably have an effect on the starport. A roll will need to be made to find out what the effect (if any) is.

#### Clubs
* Jack - Pirate crew arrive in system. Make a streetwise skill check. If passed they can be persuaded to move to a new system after a couple of traders are robbed (reduce reputation by one) but if the roll is failed they become a significant deterrent to merchants (reduce traffic by five). 
* Queen - Organised crime. Make a streetwise skill check. If failed, a sharp increase in smuggling means extra costs to hire security guards, customs inspectors and more delays - reduce traffic and reputation by five points each. But if the skill roll is made, the crime is minor and unrelated to the additional funds which have suddenly become available to finance one of the upgrades needed to bring the starport up to the next class 
* King - Incompetent leader. The government of the main world makes serious blunders that damage the reputation of the system. And consequently the reputation of the starport is reduced by 10
* Ace - War, famine, disease and / or death. Make a tactics skill check. If failed, hard times on the main world negatively impact the starport. Reduce traffic by 10

#### Diamonds
* Jack - The leader of the local merchant's trade association contacts the starport to arrange better terms for members. Make a liaison skill check. If passed the mutually beneficial agreement increases traffic by double the effect of the roll. If failed the merchants complain about unfair treatment and reputation is decreased by two.
* Queen - A megacorporation executive is considering opening a new trade route. Make an admin roll to see if this comes off. Traffic increases by five times the effect of the roll
* King - The admiral of the Navy considers anti-piracy exercises in the subsector. Make a carousing skill check. If passed the Navy will make frequent patrols and merchants are more likely to come to this system - increase traffoc and reputation by double the effect of the roll.
* Ace - A megacorporation opens a local office on the mainworld. Make a liaison roll to see what the effect on the starport is. If failed, traffic and reputation both increase by 1. If passed, points equal to five time the effect of the roll can be spilt between traffic and reputation

#### Hearts
* Jack - Official from the Scouts service visits the starport to research a location for an exploration base. Make a successful Tactics skill check to convince the scout of the benefit to locating the base in this system. If successful the repyutraion of the starport will increase by 4 points.
* Queen - A doctor arrives in system and wants to set up, or upgrade a medical facility. Succeed with a liaison skill check to have them do this at the starport - the medical facility will be upgraded to the next level
* King - A noble considers investing in the starport. Make a carousing skill check to see if they will finance one of the upgrades needed to bring the starport up to the next class
* Ace - A colony ship with large number of settlers arrives. Make an admin skill check to see if they settle on the mainworld. Traffic increases by five times the effect of the roll

#### Spades
* Jack - The city governor announces sweeping new tariffs. Make a carousing skill check. If passed the starport manages to avoid the impact. If failed the impact on trade is catastrophic, and traffic is reduced by 4
* Queen - A local mining company discovers a new source of a rare metal. Make an liaison skill check to see if this brings prosperity to the starport. Reputation and traffic both increase by double the effect of the roll
* King - A sudden and unexpected boom in productivity causes queues and delays at the starport. Make an admin skill check. If passed the schedules can be revised and traffic increases by 4. If failed traffic increases by 2 but reputation decreases by 2
* Ace - The monorail is coming! Make a streetwise skill check. If successful a monorail connection is built between the starport and the nearest city. Traffic increases by five times the effect of the roll. If the roll is failed, the connectivity improvement was a con. Reduce reputation by 3 points.
