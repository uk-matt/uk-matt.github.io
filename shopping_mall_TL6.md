Two **D66 tables** for a TL-6 mall: one for the **Customer Side** and one for the **Service Side**. 
Each entry is designed to evoke the era and support procedural generation with a mix of functional, quirky, and atmospheric locations
Roll two six-sided dice: one for the tens digit, one for the ones digit (e.g., 3 and 5 = 35).
