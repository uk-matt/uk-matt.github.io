Landing permission 

On arrival at a system's mainworld the ship must contact the starport and request landing permission. This will usually be granted by the Starport Authority (SpA) and a timeslot given to land in 30-180 minutes. This delay may be needed for other ships to land, maintenance or repair work to be done, SpA staff shifts to change-over, or many other reasons.

Descent

For each point of "size" that the mainworld has, one turn of 1000 seconds (approx 17 minutes) must be spent descending. For each of these turns the pilot must make a skill check of 9+ to avoid an "event"

Collision

Another ship will collide with the PCs ship in 1D6 rounds unless the crew can evade it. Note that no further events are rolled for on this landing / take-off

Each round the crew can throw 5+ to detect the impending collision. Some DMs may apply, depending on the cause of the collision. Throw 1D6 and refer to the table below

Throw Who is at fault?
1 - 2 Traffic control error - all rolls to detect impending collision have a -2 DM. But at least the SpA will pay for repairs, though only after an investigation taking 4D6 weeks
4 - 5 Pilot error - all rolls to detect impending collision have a -1 DM. If not established through the collision process, each pilot makes a skill check and the one with the lowest result is at fault - but there will be an investigation taking 4D6 weeks for the SpA to determine this. The vessel of the pilot determined to be at fault will receive a fine of 1D6 x Cr1000 even if no collision results - and will be liable for repair costs if one does occur
6 Roll again, and if 6 rolled a second time the other ship is making a deliberate collision attempt. Perhaps suicidal malice or maybe a lapse of judgement because of delusions, drunkeness or other impairment. The true reason may be determined through an SpA investigation taking 4D6 weeks, or further developments in the campaign - or may never be determined

When the crew does notice the danger the pilot must throw Pilot/DEX 8+ to avoid the other vessel, with the model number of the ship's computer as a DM. Look at the table below to interpret the effect of the throw.

Effect Collision damage
-6 or worse Substantial damage to both ships. Damage equivalent to a "rough landing" and each ship will need the equivalent of a major annual overhaul to stay spaceworthy
-3 to -5 The PCs ship takes substantial damage (equivalent to a "rough landing") and will need the equivalent of a major annual overhaul to stay spaceworthy. The other ship is unscathed
0 to -2 The other ship takes substantial damage (equivalent to a "rough landing") and will need the equivalent of a major annual overhaul to stay spaceworthy. The PCs ship is unscathed
1 or above Both ships are undamaged - what a relief!

Port error

Throw 1D6 and refer to additional table below. Note that no further events are rolled for on this landing / take-off

Throw Port error
1 - 3 False readings - ground control give readings that conflicts with those from the ship's sensors. This error will continue each round unless a throw of 9+ is made for the SpA personnel. PCs must choose whether to trust their own sensors or use data from the starport. If they use their own readings then limuited effect - treat as Communications Failure. If they follow the instructions from ground control then roll on the Pilot error table using the pilot's Pilot skill as a negative DM
4 - 5 Traffic misdirection - all subsequent Pilot skill throws during this landing have a DM of -2
6 Abort - landing cannot safely take place because of a groundside emergency. The ship must immediately switch to making an ascent, with a new event roll. A new landing attempt may be made in 1D6 hours

Throw 1D6 and refer to additional table below. Note that no further events are rolled for on this landing / take-off

Throw Weather experience
1 - 3 Poor visibility - no effect on a take-off. If landing must throw Pilot/EDU 9+ to avoid a "rough landing" as per Crash table below when ship reaches the planet
4 - 5 High wind - Throw 1D6. On a 1 the ship topples over on landing (see "rough landing" on Crash table below). On 2-6 roll on the Pilot Error Table with Pilot skill as a negative DM
6 Electrical storm - Throw 1D6. On 1 - 2 roll for a ship malfunction. On 3 - 4 there is a fuel leak and the ship will lose 2D6-2 x 10% of its available fuel over the next 2D6 hours. On 5 - 6 there is no significant effect on the ship
