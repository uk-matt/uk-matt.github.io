### 1. **Introduction & Overview**

-   **Planet Profile**: Basic information — name, classification (terrestrial, gas giant, ocean world, etc.), star system, climate, atmosphere, gravity, and day/year length.
    
-   **First Impressions**: A short description of what makes the planet unique or why someone would visit.
    
-   **Cultural Snapshot**: If inhabited, key features of local civilizations, customs, and etiquette.
    

----------

### 2. **Planning Your Trip**

-   **When to Go**: Best orbital season for safe travel (temperature ranges, storms, radiation exposure).
    
-   **Visas, Permits & Clearance**: Requirements for entry, planetary landing rights, or biological safety protocols.
    
-   **Health & Safety**: Hazards such as toxic air, high gravity, native microbes, or dangerous megafauna. Precautions for radiation, extreme temperatures, or pressure differences.
    
-   **Currency & Economy**: Local trade practices (credits, barter, energy-based exchanges).
    
-   **Language & Communication**: Whether translation tech is needed, or if universal signals are used.
    

----------

### 3. **Getting There & Around**

-   **Arrival**: Transportation options (orbital docks, teleportation gates, atmospheric shuttles).
    
-   **Local Transit**: Ground vehicles, maglevs, portals, flying organisms, or teleport networks.
    
-   **Navigation Tips**: Maps, AI guides, or planetary hazards (magnetic interference, shifting landscapes).
    

----------

### 4. **Regions & Destinations**

Modeled on how Earth travel guides break down countries or cities:

-   **Major Cities/Settlements**: Social hubs, political centers, or trade ports.
    
-   **Natural Wonders**: Alien landscapes (crystal forests, methane seas, bioluminescent jungles).
    
-   **Historical/Archaeological Sites**: Ruins of extinct civilizations, alien monuments, battlefields.
    
-   **Hidden Gems**: Off-the-beaten-path destinations for adventurous travelers.
    

----------

### 5. **Culture & Society**

-   **Local Inhabitants**: Physiology, customs, and behavior of native species (if any).
    
-   **Food & Drink**: Edible resources, dining etiquette, and warnings about toxic foods.
    
-   **Arts & Entertainment**: Music, theater, or sensory experiences unique to the species/planet.
    
-   **Festivals & Rituals**: Key cultural events visitors should not miss.
    

----------

### 6. **Practicalities**

-   **Accommodation**: Options ranging from biodomes to subterranean hotels to symbiotic lifeforms that "host" guests.
    
-   **Technology Access**: Availability of interstellar networks, AI assistants, or local tech quirks.
    
-   **Survival Gear**: What to pack — e.g., atmosphere converters, gravity stabilizers, protective suits.
    
-   **Laws & Etiquette**: Behavior expectations, taboos, and penalties for infractions.
    

----------

### 7. **Adventure & Activities**

-   **Outdoor Exploration**: Climbing alien mountains, diving in ammonia oceans, or sky-floating in gas giant layers.
    
-   **Extreme Sports**: Low-gravity gliding, plasma surfing, asteroid racing.
    
-   **Wildlife Encounters**: Safe viewing of alien megafauna, warnings against predatory species.
    

----------

### 8. **Traveller Tips**

-   **Common Scams or Dangers**: E.g., false navigation beacons, opportunistic traders, psychic predators.
    
-   **Emergency Contacts**: Interplanetary consulates, medical hubs, evacuation portals.
    
-   **Local Etiquette Hacks**: How to greet properly, how not to insult by accident.
    

----------

### 9. **Suggested Itineraries**

-   **Three Days on Planet X**: A quick highlights tour.
    
-   **Adventure Seeker’s Week**: Extreme landscapes and activities.
    
-   **Cultural Immersion Month**: Staying with locals, learning customs.
    

----------

### 10. **Final Section**

-   **Glossary**: Local terms, species names, units of measurement.
    
-   **Maps & Charts**: Planetary maps, orbital diagrams, gravity/atmosphere zones.
    
-   **Further Reading/Resources**: Academic guides, explorer logs, interstellar archives.
